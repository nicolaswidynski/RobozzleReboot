How it works

Program with logic, not syntax

Every puzzle is solved by dragging instructions into place — move forward, turn, paint a tile, or call one of five subroutines. Any instruction can be conditioned on the color of the tile the robot is standing on, so a handful of slots can express surprisingly deep logic.

🧭

Move & turn

Step the robot forward, or turn it left and right around the grid.

🎨

Paint tiles

Mark tiles red, green, or blue to leave yourself a trail — or a trap.

🔀

Conditions

Condition any instruction on the color of the tile the robot is on.

🔁

Subroutines

Call one of five reusable functions (F1–F5) to loop and branch.

Puzzles

Campaign + Community

Work through a curated Campaign of puzzles from Robozzle's original creators, sorted by difficulty from your first steps to genuine head-scratchers. Or dive into Community Puzzles — the full catalog, searchable and sortable, with ratings from other players.

Editor

Build your own

Design and publish your own puzzles with the built-in puzzle editor. Lay out the grid, place stars and colored tiles, test your solution, then share it with the community.
