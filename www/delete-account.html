Delete Your Account

No app install required — this works from any browser.

Enter the pseudonym and email associated with your Robozzle Reboot account. If they match an existing account, it will be permanently deleted — including your pseudonym and leaderboard entry. This can't be undone. Puzzles you've already published stay part of the community catalog; deleting your account doesn't pull them down.

Signed in with Google or Apple but never set an email, or not sure which email is on file? Contact robozzle@nwidynski.com instead and we'll take care of it.
